## 👋 About me

I'm **Kaustubh Dua**, an Electronics and Computer Science Engineering student at **Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT), Noida**.

I enjoy turning ideas into working products and learning by building. My projects span **full-stack web development, applied AI, data systems, geospatial applications, optimisation, and software engineering**.

I care about:

- clean, maintainable code
- responsive and accessible interfaces
- practical AI rather than AI for decoration
- reliable data and clear provenance
- testing, CI, documentation, and incremental improvement
